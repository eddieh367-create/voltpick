What to compare

Look at power source, whisk shape, storage, ease of cleaning and whether you want a handheld wand or a countertop unit that also heats milk.

Who should buy a handheld model

A handheld frother makes the most sense if you want quick foam, minimal storage space and a lower purchase price. If you want automatic heating and more consistent microfoam, compare countertop frothers instead.
