The short version

If your laptop lives on one desk and you use an external keyboard and mouse, the mStand is a simple one-piece aluminum riser that reviewers repeatedly call very stable. The catch is that it does exactly one height and doesn't fold, so it's the wrong pick if you need adjustability or portability.

What it's for

Raising a laptop screen closer to eye level on a fixed desk, usually with an external keyboard and mouse, or as a second screen beside a monitor.

Key specs (manufacturer and Amazon listing claims)

  • Single piece of anodized aluminum; padded rubber feet
  • Footprint 10 in wide × 7.5 in deep; raises the laptop 5.9 in (150 mm); weighs 3 lb (1.46 kg)
  • 2-inch rear cable hole; open space underneath to stash a keyboard
  • Rain Design says it fits all MacBooks including the 16" MacBook Pro; the Amazon listing says laptops less than 10.4 in deep
  • Six colors (Silver, Gold, Space Gray, Black, Midnight, Starlight); Amazon lists a 1-year manufacturer warranty
  • Rain Design claims the aluminum acts as a heat sink (manufacturer claim, not independently measured)

What independent reviewers found

  • WIRED (Mar 2025) called it "one of the most stable stands I've tested," crediting the wide 7.5 × 10 in base, and liked the space underneath for a keyboard and mouse.
  • iMore (Jul 2021, hands-on review before iMore's testing lab) praised the solid build and cord management; the main con was no adjustment. The height suited the reviewer, but her taller husband found it too low.
  • DeskDNA (Aug 2026, methodology unclear, supplementary) agrees: buy it for one permanent desk, skip it if you need adjustable height or portability.

Pros (supported by sources)

  • Very stable one-piece build, no assembly, no moving parts
  • Space underneath for a keyboard; rear cable hole
  • Finish matches MacBooks in several colors

Limitations and reasons not to buy

  • Fixed height and angle; may be too low for taller users (iMore)
  • Not portable or foldable
  • Cable hole fits one cable, not a bundle
  • You'll need an external keyboard and mouse to type comfortably
  • Laptops deeper than about 10.4 in may not fit (listing claim); check your laptop's depth first

Who it suits

Laptop users with one permanent desk and an external keyboard and mouse, especially MacBook owners who want a matching look.

Who should skip it

Anyone who works in different places, shares a desk with people of different heights, needs adjustable height, or uses a sit/stand desk where laptop position changes. WIRED names Rain Design's adjustable iLevel 2 or a folding travel stand as alternatives (not researched here).
