Best Budget Tech Gifts

Five practical electronics for gadget-minded gifters, in a modest budget band. During research for this guide, each product was listed under $75 on the official product page and/or the Amazon buy box. Prices change, so confirm the current listing before you buy.

The short version

Budget tech gifts work best when they match a job: replacing a weak wall charger, packing a power bank for travel, adding ports to a USB-C laptop, improving video calls, or adding a streaming stick to a TV. The five options below stay in a modest price band and come from current official and/or Amazon listings. This is a shortlist, not a ranked test.

100W USB-C charger

Anker Nano Charger (100W) with USB-C Cable

Anker Nano Charger (100W)

Anker's official page lists this as the Anker Nano Charger (100W) with USB-C Cable, model A2679: a single USB-C port with a 100W maximum output, dimensions of 2.36 × 1.61 × 1.14 inches, and a weight of 4.23 oz. The official listing includes a 6 ft USB-C cable and describes a foldable plug. 100W is aimed at USB-C laptops and other devices that can accept that much power; a given phone, tablet, or laptop will only draw what it is designed to take. Confirm that the recipient's device uses USB-C charging. The Amazon listing researched for this guide (ASIN B0D91GKXLP) was sold by AnkerDirect and also listed the 6 ft cable in the box.

Best for someone still using an undersized charger with a USB-C laptop or a mixed phone-and-laptop bag. Skip it if they only need a compact 30W phone charger (see VoltPick's stocking stuffers guide), or if they need a multi-port charger.

10,000mAh power bank

Anker Nano Power Bank (30W, Built-In USB-C Cable)

Anker Nano Power Bank 10,000mAh

Anker's official page lists this as the Anker Nano Power Bank (30W, Built-In USB-C Cable), model A1259: a 10,000mAh pack with a built-in USB-C cable, a USB-C port, and a USB-A port. Official dimensions are 4.09 × 2.06 × 1.02 inches, with a weight of 7.58 oz. Anker rates a single port at up to 30W and describes 30W bidirectional charging over the USB-C cable. This is a mid-size pack, not a high-capacity laptop brick. If it might be carried on a plane, check the airline's current rules for spare batteries. The Amazon listing researched for this guide (ASIN B0C9CJKCH3) was sold by AnkerDirect. Several colors are sold; confirm the color on the listing you use.

Best for travel or everyday top-ups when a built-in cable is convenient. Skip it if they want a much larger capacity, a magnetic wireless pack, or a pack that can run a power-hungry laptop for long sessions.

USB-C hub

Anker 555 USB-C Hub (8-in-1)

Anker 555 USB-C Hub

Anker's official page lists this as the Anker 555 USB-C Hub (8-in-1), model A8383. Official ports include HDMI at up to 4K@60Hz, two USB-A data ports and one USB-C data port at up to 10 Gbps, a 1 Gbps Ethernet port, and SD and microSD slots. Official pass-through charging is up to 100W in, with up to 85W to the laptop after the hub's own use; a charger is not included. Official size is 4.76 × 2.17 × 0.6 inches and about 4.4–4.5 oz. Anker notes that the hub's USB-C data port does not support video output, that the USB data ports do not support charging, and that the hub is incompatible with Nintendo Switch and Apple USB SuperDrive. The official compatibility list is limited and includes older MacBook, iPad Pro, XPS, and Pixelbook models; check the current official notes against the recipient's laptop. The Amazon listing researched for this guide (ASIN B087QZVQJX) was sold by AnkerDirect.

Best for a USB-C laptop that needs a monitor, wired Ethernet, USB-A accessories, or a card reader. Skip it if the recipient does not have a USB-C port, needs DisplayPort or dual monitors beyond what this hub provides, or needs the hub's ports themselves to charge accessories.

1080p webcam

Logitech Brio 100

Logitech Brio 100

Logitech's official Brio 100 page describes a Full HD 1080p webcam with auto-light balance (RightLight), an integrated privacy shutter, and a built-in microphone. Logitech says it is compatible with most calling platforms such as Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and Zoom, and with Windows, macOS, and ChromeOS. This is a 1080p camera, not a 4K webcam, and the built-in mic is a convenience feature rather than a replacement for a dedicated microphone. Official Graphite was in stock on Logitech's U.S. store during research. The Amazon listing researched for this guide (ASIN B0BXGCRRLW) was sold by Madel1, not a Logitech-named storefront. Prefer Logitech's official page if you want to buy direct, and if you use Amazon, confirm the current seller.

Best for straightforward video calls on a laptop or desktop that needs a simple plug-in camera and a privacy shutter. Skip it if they need 4K, a more flexible mount, or a separate microphone, or if you cannot confirm the seller on the listing you use.

4K streaming stick

Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K

Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K

The Amazon listing researched for this guide is the current Fire TV Stick 4K (ASIN B0G4XRP464), shipped from and sold by Amazon.com. Amazon describes 4K streaming with HDR10+, Wi-Fi 6 support, a new Fire TV remote, and HDMI setup. The listing also describes Direct Power, meaning the stick can be powered from the TV rather than a separate wall adapter; confirm that detail on the current page and on the recipient's TV. 4K and HDR features require a compatible television. This is Amazon's Fire TV software, not Google TV or Roku, and the recipient will need an HDMI port, internet access, and an Amazon account. Streaming apps and subscriptions are separate from the device.

Best for adding a dedicated streaming stick and remote to a compatible HDMI TV. Skip it if the TV already has a streaming platform they like, or if they specifically want Google TV or Roku rather than Fire TV.

How to choose among these

ChargingGive the 100W Nano charger if a USB-C laptop is in the picture. Give the 10,000mAh power bank if they are often away from an outlet. They complement each other, but you do not need both unless that is the intent.
Laptop portsThe 555 hub is the pick when a USB-C laptop needs HDMI, Ethernet, USB-A, or a card reader. Bring a separate charger if they want pass-through charging.
Calls or the living roomThe Brio 100 is the simple 1080p camera. The Fire TV Stick 4K is for a television, not a desk.
A smaller budgetFor under $25 tech, see the stocking stuffers guide, including the 30W Nano charger and Tapo P115 plugs.
