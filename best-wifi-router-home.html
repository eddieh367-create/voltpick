What to Look for in an Under Desk Treadmill

Thickness First

Before speed or features, check the thickness against your actual desk's clearance at standing height. A pad that's an inch too thick means you can't reach your keyboard comfortably — this is the single most common regret with walking pad purchases.

Noise Level

If you take calls during the day, this matters more than almost anything else. Look specifically for reviews mentioning video call use, not just general "quiet" claims.

Shock Absorption

A hard belt is fine for 10 minutes and miserable for 2 hours. If you plan to actually use it most of the work day, don't skip this spec to save money.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you actually work while using a walking pad?
Yes, at typing and mousing speeds (roughly 1–2.5 mph) most people can type and read normally. Video calls work fine too, especially with the display muted. Anything requiring fine motor precision, like detailed design work, is easier standing still.
How much desk clearance do you need?
Measure your standing-height desk clearance and compare it to the pad's listed thickness, then add an inch or two of buffer for comfortable stepping. Most people find 4–5 inches of pad thickness works under a typical adjustable standing desk.
Is a walking pad worth it if I already have a standing desk?
If you already stand for part of your day, a walking pad is a natural next step rather than a separate purchase — you're using the same standing time to also log steps, without carving out extra time for a workout.
