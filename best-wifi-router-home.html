What to compare

Match the router to your internet speed, home size, number of devices and whether you need mesh expansion. A more expensive router will not fix a poor modem connection or an internet plan that is slower than your actual needs.

When mesh makes more sense

Large or multi-story homes with dead zones may benefit more from a mesh system than a single high-powered router.

Security

Use current firmware, a strong administrator password and WPA2 or WPA3 security where supported.
