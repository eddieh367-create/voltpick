Crypto for Beginners

Risk notice: Cryptocurrency can be highly volatile and losses can be substantial. This page is general education, not investment advice.

What cryptocurrency is

Cryptocurrencies are digital assets recorded on blockchain networks. Different networks have different rules, use cases, transaction costs and security models.

Exchanges and wallets are different

An exchange is a service used to buy, sell or trade assets. A wallet is a tool used to control blockchain addresses and private keys. Holding assets on an exchange introduces platform risk; self-custody introduces the responsibility of protecting keys and recovery phrases.

Common beginner risks

Price volatility, scams, irreversible transfers, compromised accounts, fake tokens and lost recovery phrases are all material risks. Never send funds because of pressure, guaranteed-return claims or unsolicited messages.

Start with security

Use strong unique passwords, multifactor authentication and verified app or website addresses. Keep recovery phrases offline and never share them with anyone.
