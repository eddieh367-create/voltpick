Privacy Policy

How VoltPick handles analytics, affiliate-link measurement and information you choose to provide.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

VoltPick is an independent website operated under the VoltPick brand. This policy explains information that may be processed when you use the site.

Google Analytics

VoltPick uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and page use. Google Analytics may process browser and device information, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed, referral source and interaction events. Google may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy terms.

Affiliate-link measurement

VoltPick uses direct affiliate links to participating retailers. The site records an analytics event when a visitor clicks a retailer button so we can understand which products and pages are useful. The event can include the product name, affiliate partner and destination URL.

Affiliate partners

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. VoltPick may also participate in other affiliate programs. When you follow an affiliate link, the destination retailer may use cookies or other attribution technologies under its own privacy policy.

Information you provide

If you contact VoltPick through a third-party platform such as Pinterest, the information you submit is handled through that platform and according to its privacy terms. VoltPick does not currently operate an on-site account system or newsletter signup that requests personal profile information.

Third-party links

VoltPick links to websites it does not control, including retailers and social platforms. Once you leave VoltPick, the privacy practices of the destination site apply.

Children

VoltPick is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's analytics, affiliate relationships or data practices change. The revision date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method listed on the Contact page.
