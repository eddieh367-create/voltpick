The short version

If you want a heavy, architectural aluminum home for documents and small desk objects, the Industry Desk Set is Craighill's offering in that category. The important buying detail is that "set" here means a three-piece family. On Craighill's official product page, the In/Out Box, Monument Letter Holder, and Facility Catchall are individual purchase options, not a single three-piece bundle. Buy the pieces you need; skip it if you expected one box with all three, or if you mainly need inexpensive, high-capacity storage.

What it's for

Craighill positions the pieces this way: the In/Out Box as a "halfway-home" for important documents and their comings and goings; the Monument Letter Holder for keeping letters, invoices, or other envelopes upright and at hand; and the Facility Catchall as a home for pens, clips, magnets, or other errant objects.

Key specs (manufacturer claims)

The following comes from Craighill's official Industry Desk Set page, opened 2026-11-13. Craighill lists one finish: clear anodized 5052 aluminum. The product page does not offer other colors.

  • Construction: shaped from ¼" sheets of brake press-formed aluminum; Craighill also uses the phrase "brake-pressed aluminum construction"
  • Material and finish: clear anodized 5052 aluminum (official Craighill specification)
  • In/Out Box: 12.25" × 10" × 3" / 4 lbs 13 oz
  • Monument Letter Holder: 8" × 5.25" × 4.25" / 3 lbs 9 oz
  • Facility Catchall: 10" × 4" × 2" / 2 lbs 1 oz
  • Care: wipe clean with a soft, damp cloth and dry thoroughly; Craighill says the product will show signs of wear over time and treats that as a natural quality of the material
  • Collaboration: designed with Bestcase; Craighill calls this their first collaboration

Some retailer and Amazon listings describe the metal as 3003 aluminum and say the pieces are made in New York, USA. Craighill's official specification is 5052, and the official product page does not state a made-in location. That conflict is unresolved here; this guide uses the official 5052 specification.

What reviewers and other sources say

  • IMBOLDN (Mar 20, 2025) described the collection as modular and summarized the same three pieces. The article is a product write-up, not a hands-on lab test. It also referred to the collaborator as "Best Made" and "Best Case"; Craighill's official partner name is Bestcase.
  • On Craighill's product page, verified buyer Louise T. called the pieces heavy and precisely machined, said the metal was "brighter than expected but also matte," wished the letter holder had a third slot (she described two sections), and said the catchall resembled "a raised platform or pedestal" and could be deeper. These are attributed buyer comments, not VoltPick tests.
  • Verified buyer Daniel S., reviewing the letter holder on the same page, praised the form and called it "minimalist industrial." Independent long-form reviews beyond Craighill's own site and a short IMBOLDN write-up were not found.

Pros (supported by sources)

  • Official construction is substantial: ¼-inch brake press-formed, clear-anodized aluminum
  • Three distinct jobs (document box, upright letter holder, catchall) so you can buy only what you need
  • Matching family of pieces if you want a coordinated desk
  • Craighill states a general "For Life Guarantee" on the product page (policy details should be checked on Craighill before buying)

Limitations and reasons not to buy

  • Not sold as one three-piece bundle on Craighill's official product page; each piece is a separate purchase
  • The pieces are large and heavy for desk accessories (official weights add up to more than 10 pounds if you buy all three)
  • A verified Craighill buyer said the letter holder has two sections, not three, and that the catchall is shallow
  • Official alloy (5052) disagrees with some Amazon and retailer listings (3003); the official spec is used here, but the conflict is unresolved
  • Independent testing is scarce
  • As observed 2026-11-13, Craighill showed the In/Out Box out of stock, and no verified Amazon listing was found for that piece

Who it suits

People furnishing a permanent home office who want a heavy, sculptural aluminum organizer and are willing to choose individual pieces. It also fits someone who only needs the letter holder or catchall and does not want to buy a full set.

Who should skip it

Anyone who wants one inexpensive, complete desk-organizer kit in a single box; anyone who needs a deep catchall or more than two letter slots; anyone with a small desk who cannot spare the official footprints; or anyone who needs the In/Out Box immediately, given the stock and Amazon gaps observed on 2026-11-13.
