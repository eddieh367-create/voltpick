What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing pays a publisher a commission when a reader follows a properly attributed link and completes a qualifying action or purchase.

The basic funnel

A useful content funnel is: search or social discovery → helpful article or buying guide → clearly disclosed affiliate link → retailer. The content should add value before asking the reader to click.

Disclosure is part of the model

Readers should be able to tell when a commercial relationship exists. VoltPick uses an Amazon Associate disclosure and identifies sponsored affiliate links in the site markup.

Traffic is not enough

High traffic with low buyer intent may earn less than a smaller audience reading a focused buying guide. Content should match a real decision such as choosing a kitchen scale, router or air fryer.

Track without hiding the destination

VoltPick's current structure uses direct retailer links and Google Analytics click events rather than a separate redirect service between the site and Amazon.

Measure the right things

Track page traffic, outbound affiliate clicks, conversion rate and commission by product category. Use those numbers to decide which guides deserve more content and Pinterest promotion.
