What matters most

Capacity: A larger basket is useful for families but takes more counter space. Cleaning: Removable dishwasher-safe parts can matter more than extra cooking presets. Controls: Look for a temperature range and timer that match what you actually cook. Value: Do not pay more for functions you will not use.

Who should consider a smaller model

If you cook for one or two people, have limited counter space or primarily reheat small portions, a compact air fryer can be a better fit than a 6-quart model.
