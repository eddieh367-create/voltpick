Best Stocking Stuffers Under $25

Six compact, practical gifts for home, kitchen, and everyday tech. Every product on this page was listed under $25 on the official product page and/or the Amazon buy box during research for this guide. Prices change, so confirm the current listing before you buy. For a last-minute gift, check the listing's delivery date rather than assuming it will arrive in time.

Affiliate disclosure: VoltPick may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. VoltPick has not tested these products hands-on. This guide summarizes manufacturer specifications and merchant listings linked below.

The short version

Stocking stuffers work best when they are small, useful, and easy to choose without knowing someone's exact style. The six options below cover a phone or tablet charger (cable not included on the listing researched), a everyday silicone spatula, kitchen scissors with a herb stripper, a jar opener, a compact seven-function pocket knife, and a two-pack of energy-monitoring smart plugs. This is a shortlist, not a ranked test. Specs come from the manufacturer or the current merchant listing.

Amazon links use VoltPick's affiliate tag. Manufacturer pages linked as "official" or "direct" are not affiliate links.

Compact USB-C charger

Anker Nano Charger (30W)

Anker Nano Charger (30W)

Anker's official page lists this as the Anker Nano Charger (30W), model A2147: a single USB-C port with a 30W maximum output, measuring 1.12 × 1.12 × 1.39 inches. Anker describes ActiveShield 2.0 temperature monitoring and a compact design meant to sit beside other plugs on a power strip. The Amazon listing researched for this guide was Phantom Black, sold by AnkerDirect, and stated that a cable is not included. 30W is intended for phones, tablets, and smaller laptops that accept a 30W USB-C input; it is not a replacement for a higher-wattage laptop charger. Confirm compatibility with the recipient's device, and plan to add a cable if they do not already have one.

Best for a last-minute tech stocking stuffer that stays small. Skip it if the recipient needs 65W or 100W for a USB-C laptop, or if you want a charger-and-cable kit in one box.

Silicone spatula

GIR Ultimate Spatula

GIR Ultimate Spatula

GIR's official spatula page describes a one-piece tool made from platinum-cured, pharmaceutical-grade silicone with a proprietary inner core and no seams or joints. Official details for the Ultimate size are 11.0 in L × 1.9 in W × 0.7 in H, heat resistant to 425°F, dishwasher safe, nonstick-safe, and BPA-, BPS-, and phthalate-free. GIR also sells Skinny and Mini sizes; this guide points to the Ultimate size. The Amazon listing researched for this guide was Studio White, sold by GIR: Get It Right. Several colors are sold; confirm the color and size on the listing you use.

Best for home cooks who will actually use a sturdy scraping and mixing spatula. Skip it if they already have a spatula they like, or if they really need a turner for flipping food rather than a spatula.

Kitchen scissors

OXO Good Grips Kitchen and Herb Scissors

OXO Kitchen and Herb Scissors

OXO's official page describes the Kitchen and Herb Scissors (SKU 1072121) as a tool for cutting meat, vegetables, twine, and more. Official specifications are 8.75" × 3.5" × .5", with a stainless-steel build, soft handles with cushioned pads, a herb stripper above the handles, and micro-serrated blades that separate for cleaning. The matching Amazon listing researched for this guide (ASIN B000KILLXM, model 1072121) was shipped from and sold by Amazon.com. These are general kitchen scissors, not OXO's separate poultry shears.

Best for someone who will use one pair of scissors for everyday kitchen prep, including herbs. Skip it if they specifically need spring-loaded poultry shears for bones and joints.

Jar opener

OXO Good Grips Jar Opener with Base Pad

OXO Jar Opener with Base Pad

OXO's official page describes the Jar Opener with Base Pad (SKU 1173600) as a tool that grips jars and lids so the user can twist. Official dimensions are 9" × 5" × 1", with a stainless-steel build and a non-slip base pad that is placed under the jar; OXO's instructions are to slide the opener over the lid and twist counter-clockwise. The Amazon listing researched for this guide (ASIN B002DWA6KM, model 1173600) was sold by Hour Loop, not an OXO-named storefront, and the page also pointed to a newer model. If you use Amazon, confirm the current seller and that the SKU matches 1173600. Prefer OXO's official page if you want to buy direct.

Best for a practical helper aimed at tight lids. Skip it if they want an electric opener, or if you cannot confirm the seller and model on the listing you use.

Compact pocket knife

Victorinox Classic SD

Victorinox Classic SD

Victorinox's official Classic SD Classic Colors page lists a Swiss-made pocket knife with seven functions: a small blade, scissors, a nail file with a 2.5 mm screwdriver, a key ring, tweezers, and a toothpick. Official dimensions for the Classic Colors models are 2.3 in long, 0.7 in wide, and 0.4 in high, with ABS/cellidor scales and a Victorinox lifetime warranty. It is a compact keychain tool, not a full-size pocket knife. The Amazon listing researched for this guide was the Style Icon (Red) Classic SD (ASIN B092DZCM42), shipped from and sold by Amazon.com. Several colors are sold; confirm the color on the listing you use. Check current local rules and, if it might be packed for travel, the airline's current rules before gifting a blade.

Best for someone who will actually use a tiny everyday tool on a key ring. Skip it if a blade is the wrong gift, if they need a larger knife, or if you prefer the Alox or other higher-priced Classic SD versions, which are a different price band.

Smart plug two-pack

Tapo P115 Mini Smart Wi-Fi Plug (2-pack)

Tapo P115 2-pack

TP-Link's official Tapo P115 page describes a mini smart plug with energy monitoring, schedules and timers, remote control through the Tapo app, optional voice control with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Samsung SmartThings, and no separate hub. Official specifications include 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (plus Bluetooth for setup only), dimensions of 2.6 × 1.5 × 1.6 in, a 15A / 1800W maximum load for general use or resistive loads, and a compact shape meant not to block adjacent sockets. Best for a small home-tech gift when the recipient already uses, or is open to, app-controlled plugs. Skip it if they only have 5 GHz Wi-Fi, do not want a cloud-connected device, or need a higher-load outlet than the official rating allows.

How to choose among these

Everyday techGive the Anker 30W charger if they use USB-C phones or tablets and still rely on a bulky brick. Give the Tapo 2-pack if they want schedules or energy monitoring on lamps or similar low-to-moderate loads.
Kitchen prepThe GIR spatula and OXO scissors are frequently used tools that do not add an appliance. The jar opener is the more specific helper for tight lids.
A tiny everyday toolThe Classic SD is a compact keychain gift. Skip it when a blade is inappropriate, and check current rules if it might travel.
Last-minute deliveryThese are widely sold, compact gifts, but delivery dates change. Confirm the current listing's arrival estimate before you count on a stocking deadline.
